**Q1. Reconstruction: what R1–R3, R5–R6 do and do not show.** For D1 = ‖Δθ‖ and D2, the reconstruction is not an artefact — it is expected by mechanism. The Adam update rule (Kingma & Ba, arXiv:1412.6980) gives Δθ_t = −η_t·m̂_t/(√v̂_t + ε) elementwise, so ‖Δθ_t‖ is a deterministic function of (η_t, m̂_t, v̂_t), all of which sit in B_strong. The observed held-out R² of 0.94–0.99 for Ση‖m̂‖ is therefore the mean-field identity plus approximation error, not an empirical surprise [my analysis; the dossier itself gestures at this via Kosson et al., arXiv:2305.17212, dossier-supplied]. But the converse claim — "the clocks carry no information beyond telemetry" — is stronger than the data. R² 0.86–0.99 leaves 1–14% of variance unreconstructed, and whether that residual predicts forgetting is precisely H1, which was never analysed. The defensible claim is: "≥86–99% of the rate's variance is reconstructible from cheap telemetry, held-out"; "no information" is not established.

Artefacts I consider plausible, with the check that rules each out:

1. **Near-duplicate competitor.** Ση‖m̂‖ is not independent of D1 — it is its first-order determinant, so "reconstruction by it" is close to tautological. Check: compute the per-step elementwise residual r_t = ‖Δθ_t‖ − η_t‖m̂_t/(√v̂_t+ε)‖ and report Var(r)/Var(‖Δθ‖); then test whether r adds anything to T1. Also note the dual reconstruction in R3: C10c (a purely functional, same-cost competitor) reconstructs D1 at 0.88–0.93 in F2/F3/F5 while ΔN alone fails — the clock is reconstructible from *either* optimizer state *or* functional change, both cheap. That dual redundancy is itself the finding.
2. **Leakage between training and test runs.** R1's leave-one-run-out blocks same-run leakage; R3's leave-one-family-out is the correct stronger check and D2 stays at 0.98–0.99, so family-specific leakage is limited. Residual risk: all runs share one world, one task set, one childhood, so a GBDT can absorb world-specific structure. Check: train the reconstruction on runs from a *different world seed* (fresh random facts) and test on the original runs; require R² ≥ 0.90 to exclude world leakage. Also multi-seed within family (R3 has n = 1 per family).
3. **Window length.** R2 already shows 23% relative error at 64–127-step intervals vs 8% at long ones, and the LM arm uses 8-step windows while the synthetic arm uses 64–1,280 — the R² values are not comparable across arms. Check: fix one window length (e.g., 64 steps) for all G2 tests; recompute the LM reconstruction at 64-step windows; report R² as a function of window length.
4. **Rate vs cumulative.** Since ρ = Δc_e/ΔN and ΔN ∈ B_strong, reconstructing ρ reconstructs Δc_e — no artefact there. But the gate should test the same object as H1: check direct reconstruction of Δc_e over the same (t0, t1] intervals used for T1, at matched granularity, rather than a rate at a different granularity.
5. **Synthetic tasks.** Arbitrary key→value facts with no semantic overlap; D3 on R measures functional drift on clean reference items. Check: the LM arm with D3 against its *missing* same-cost competitor (the C10c analogue on the 128 stored probes — dossier §6 admits it was never computed); and/or a synthetic family with shared vocabulary across tasks. If the F6 exception does not appear on the LM arm, it may be synthetic-specific.
6. **Threshold arbitrariness.** At G2 = 0.90 all four candidates fail (R1); at 0.95, D1_rel (0.96–0.98) and D2 pass. Check: pre-register a threshold-sensitivity report at 0.85/0.90/0.95/0.99 before any confirmatory use of G2.

**Q2. Is a confirmatory H1 still worth running given G2 fails?** First, the gate's logic is flawed: R² ≥ 0.90 reconstruction does not make H1 unidentifiable. The residual (up to 14% of variance) could still predict T1, and the H1 statistic is the *incremental* R² for T1, not the clock's own R². "G2 fails ⇒ H1 not identifiable" is a non sequitur [my analysis]. Nevertheless, for D1/D1_rel/D2 a confirmatory H1 is not worth running as specified, for two different reasons: (i) mechanism — the clock is a function of optimizer state by construction, so any predictive content is already in B_strong up to approximation error; the only open object is the residual, a narrow question; (ii) power — one seed per family cannot detect an incremental R² consistent with a ≤14% residual. If the residual question is to be closed at all, run it as a residual test with ≥8 seeds per family (48 runs ≈ 4 h CPU): H: the per-step residual r adds < 0.02 held-out R² for T1 beyond B_strong; pass: incremental R² < 0.02 in ≥6/8 seeds per family; fail: ≥ 0.05. Expected outcome is a trivial pass, which is exactly why it belongs in a closure report, not in Stage A.

Scientific content left: (1) the identity as the primary result — parameter-distance clocks are optimizer telemetry up to first order, quantified at held-out R² 0.86–0.99 across two arms; (2) D3 in F6 (Q3); (3) the LM-arm D3 vs its same-cost competitor; (4) FOREVER trigger equivalence (Q4). Reporting: a negative result *with mechanism*, pre-registered as descriptive — "held-out R² of the rate from B_strong = X; residual variance = Y; no clock–forgetting analysis performed" — not a null-hypothesis test.

**Q3. The open case: D3 vs C10c in F6.** Currently undecidable, and I suspect partly artefact. The F6 exception rests on one seed per family; GBDT still reconstructs D3 at 0.81 in F6 and C10c at 0.59, so "no cheap scalar reconstructs D3" is true only for the two scalars tried, not for the full ladder. Moreover C10c and D3 are near-duplicates by construction (same probes, same forward passes; √JS over the output distribution vs |Δ loss| on the trained token are different functionals but correlate 0.98–0.99 in clean families). Under 30% label noise, full-distribution drift can plausibly decouple from loss-on-R change — a real mechanism — but it could equally be one noisy seed.

Decisive preregisterable package (all CPU unless noted):

- **T1 — multi-seed F6 reconstruction + H1.** 8 seeds F6 + 8 seeds F1 (control), corrected code, full ladder plus same-cost alternative functional summaries (mean KL(p_t‖p_{t+1}), mean |Δ log p(argmax)|, Δ output entropy on R). Pre-fixed: (a) G2-F6: GBDT leave-one-seed-out R² for the D3 rate < 0.90 in ≥6/8 F6 seeds; (b) H1-F6: incremental held-out within-run R² for T1 when the D3 residual (after GBDT) is added to B_strong + C10c ≥ 0.05 with permutation p < 0.05 (1,000 within-run permutations). Fail: GBDT ≥ 0.90 in ≥6/8 seeds, or incremental R² < 0.02. Cost: 16 runs ≈ 1.5 h CPU + ~2 h analysis.
- **T2 — noise dose–response.** F6 with label noise ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5}, 4 seeds each (16 runs ≈ 1.5 h). H: unique variance of D3 (1 − GBDT R²) increases monotonically with noise. Pass: Spearman ρ = 1.0 across levels and unique variance at 0.3 ≥ 2× that at 0. Fail: no monotone trend. This separates "real functional signal under noise" from "seed artefact".
- **T3 — LM-arm same-cost competitor.** Compute the C10c analogue from stored probe log-probs in lmtech1/2 (mean |Δ(−log p(random answer word))| over the 128 probes). H: C10c_LM reconstructs the D3 rate at held-out R² ≥ 0.90, as in synthetic F1–F3. If < 0.90, the exception generalises to LM and 3 new logged seeds are warranted (0.75 h GPU). Cost now: ~0.5 h analysis, no GPU.

If T1–T3 pass, the question is genuine: under label noise, functional output drift on held-out probes has variance no cheap scalar captures, and H1-F6 (does it predict forgetting?) becomes the confirmatory test. If they fail, close D3 too and the whole line is closed.

**Q4. FOREVER: fair claims and the incontestable experiment.** Fairly claimable from R5–R8 (all descriptive, 3 seeds, one configuration): (i) the 8-step-window τ rate is reconstructible from Ση‖m̂‖ at held-out R² 0.94–0.96 across fp16/bf16 and α16/α32 (R5/R6/R9) and from full telemetry at 0.976–0.988; the pre-fixed schedule-only prediction failed (Ση: 0.58–0.69 < 0.80); (ii) at phase level τ ≈ Ση × duration, but steps alone give the same R² — a duration artefact — and the per-phase *rate* is unexplained by Ση (0.09–0.18); (iii) a schedule-only clock (cumulative η normalised by its calibration value) triggers d = 2 at median −1 step vs τ and d = 4 ~9 steps earlier — but this is computed on stored arrays of the *same* runs, i.e. an arithmetic equivalence of trigger sequences, not independent scheduler runs; (iv) mechanism: Δθ = −η m̂/(√v̂+ε) (Kingma & Ba, arXiv:1412.6980), so τ is a deterministic function of optimizer state to first order; (v) implementation caveat: every phase is a fresh `Trainer` (fresh AdamW, schedule, GradScaler), replay steps are excluded from τ and the reference point moves — so τ as implemented is a per-phase, replay-excluded path length, not a global monotone clock. Not claimable: benchmark gains (SuperNI not reproduced); any clock→forgetting relation (never analysed); generalisation beyond Qwen3-0.6B, synthetic facts, commit bbeb549 with the listed deviations; any stronger sense of "model-centric time".

The incontestable experiment is **T4 — scheduler equivalence**: run the FOREVER replay scheduler driven by (a) τ, (b) cumulative Ση‖m̂‖, (c) schedule-only cumulative η normalised by calibration, (d) step count; 5 seeds each; compare full trigger sequences and T1 forgetting plus final accuracy. Pre-fixed pass (clock redundant): median |Δ trigger step| ≤ 2 for all d ∈ {1,2,4} and forgetting difference ≤ 0.02; fail: any median difference > 5 steps or forgetting difference > 0.05. Cost: 20 × 15 min = 5 h GPU; an offline pre-check simulating triggers from the stored per-step τ/Ση arrays costs 0.1 h and should be done first, since after the first replay the counterfactual trajectories diverge and later triggers may differ from the early-trigger medians reported in R7.

A short descriptive note **now** is justified [my opinion, confidence medium]: the claims are descriptive, already collected, robust across three LM configurations (R5/R6/R9) and two arms, and the note would contain no T1 analysis, so the confirmatory H1 line stays clean. Restrict it to (i)–(v) above, state the 3-seed/one-model-size limitation and the R8 validity caveats, and explicitly disclaim benchmark and forgetting claims. The dossier's literature check (13 citing papers, none auditing the clock — unverified by me) means no published audit exists to duplicate.

**Q6. Red team.** The strongest case that these results are wrong or trivial: (1) *Triviality* — for D1/D2/τ the reconstruction is the Adam mean-field identity, a mathematical fact requiring no experiment; R1/R3/R5/R6 for these candidates merely quantify approximation error.
