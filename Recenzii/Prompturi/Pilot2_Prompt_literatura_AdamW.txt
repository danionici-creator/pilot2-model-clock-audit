CONTEXT
FOREVER (Feng et al., ACL 2026, arXiv:2601.03938) schedules replay in LLM continual learning on "model-centric time": tau = sum_t ||Theta_t - Theta_{t-1}||_2 over the trainable LoRA factors. Its only alternative clock in the paper is the step count. In its public code (github.com/WoodScene/FOREVER), every training phase builds a new Hugging Face Trainer: AdamW with weight decay 0, a linear learning-rate schedule with 10 warmup steps that restarts each phase, and optimizer moments reset each phase.

Our preliminary finding (synthetic continual-learning runs, AdamW, small Transformer, 5 technical runs, untuned models, blind to any forgetting outcome): the interval rate of tau is reconstructed from cheap optimizer telemetry with out-of-run R^2 = 0.98-0.99. A single scalar, the learning-rate-weighted first-moment norm sum_t eta_t ||m_hat_t||, plus duration, gives R^2 = 0.94. Learning-rate-weighted steps alone (sum_t eta_t) do not: the update size varies with gradient signal-to-noise ratio. A functional distance (change of outputs on a fixed probe set) is less reconstructible (R^2 0.91-0.96; 0.85 from the single scalar).
