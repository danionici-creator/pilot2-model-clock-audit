## B: checked and found correct / bounded conclusions

- g2_preview, td_audit, tech4_analysis and tb_part1_g2 fit their StandardScaler only inside training pipelines. Held-out masks are by run/family, not randomly split overlapping triplets. Overlap within a training run is not test contamination. GBDT hyperparameters are fixed in these scripts; no test-fitted scaling or explicit test-driven tuning was found.
- lm_forever_g2 and lm_d3_competitors hold out run names within each configuration; sni_analysis separately holds out runs and tasks. Task holdout still shares training trajectories, so it is weaker than independent-run transfer. td_audit's cross-configuration test is distinct from its leave-seed-out test.
- T-B excludes rate_ce_* and rate_Disp_* and uses rate features rather than C6 endpoint summaries. C10c/p/h are shared-probe competitors, not literal copies of D3: target-label loss, target probability and entropy changes versus full-distribution distance. Their shared measurement source limits an interpretation as independent cheap optimizer telemetry. LM L1, the near duplicate, is explicitly excluded from the verdict.
- T-A paired t intervals, ±0.02 margin, superiority rule and E1/E2 definitions agree with the Romanian preregistration. T-B's 6/8 threshold at noise 0.3, strict dose monotonicity and gate blocking H1 agree. SuperNI's mean R² in both schemes and paired relative-loss ±5% interval agree. rep_verdict applies the superseding version-3 common-adapter pairing and strict >2/<−2 rules correctly; stage 2 is optional and descriptive.
- Port inspection: 24-step calibration; thresholds 1,2,4,7,15,30,60,90,120 times the day; fresh optimizer/scheduler per phase; EMA coefficient .05; beta clipped .5–3; replay excluded from tau; reference moved after replay; task-start regularization anchor retained. Beta remains based on true tau in the clock-swap port. Continuous data stream, precision, replay-memory and training-duration changes are declared. Exact fidelity to the absent upstream implementation is unverified.
- Clock variant changes the replay counter and adds logging; mu still uses delta_t. However it counts lr times stale m on skipped optimizer updates; this is acknowledged by its post hoc zero-update diagnostic. A zero update alone does not prove GradScaler skip (warmup zero LR also exists). Hard-coded beta1=.9 and first-group LR limit portability.
- Synthetic agent uses explicit AdamW moments and no future gradient in its current update; D1 is algebraically tied to that update, not independently generated latent experience. D3 and competitors share probe evaluations. This can make reconstruction easy without statistical train/test leakage; conclusions must remain implementation-specific.
- Post hoc memory summary reconstructs memory sampling with seed*10+4, excludes task 9 and labels t intervals descriptive. Only tau/7301 validates all facts; the other cells are memory-only CPU rescoring. Noise summary separates greedy from sampled SD; three sampled evaluations per cell cannot establish a population noise bound. Tie diagnostic's supplied output can be checked, but raw-logit provenance cannot be certified without reproducing the absent model/software stack (output_scores may be processed generation scores).

- First reconstruction table checked against the newly printed tech-G2 result, including D3 .919/.960 OLS and .907/.950 GBDT, dN-only failures, CV .374–.734 and mean cheap-scalar .935/.919/.987/.846. Rounded table entries agree. C6-free T-D, T-B, T-C, four LM configurations and SuperNI summaries reproduce their logs. The report discloses family-transfer failures and does not substitute them for the prespecified within-family G2 rule.
- Original and common-start OP/BWT/dbpedia contrasts, trigger table, post hoc memory and evaluation-noise summaries reproduce. Common-start dbpedia differences have opposite signs; this meets the version-3 “not robustly reproduced” rule. It is not a causal demonstration of no effect. The report explicitly acknowledges noise versus interaction and calls original-code ±2 points descriptive rather than a statistical equivalence test.
- The report distinguishes H1 not tested from H1 refuted, identifies the gate as an operational convention and admits untested residual predictive information (§6). A reconstruction threshold of .90 is not a theorem of conditional independence. `check_c.py` constructs y=x+.1z with x orthogonal to z: reconstruction R²=.990099, yet the residual perfectly predicts outcome z. This is relevant to Perspective D01, not a reason to violate the prespecified stopping rule after the fact.
- §5.3's zero-decay AdamW expression is correct in ideal arithmetic for a committed step; first and second moments plus LR determine that ideal update. It does not establish that a single moment norm suffices. The report properly says beta still uses true update norms, so the implemented clock swap does not remove the parameter copy. “Free” means no additional state source, not zero O(P) reduction work or zero temporary allocation; runtime savings were not measured.
- Important limitations already present: local rather than deposited preregistration, adaptive design sequence, unpreserved historical code, small samples, one hardware setup, synthetic strong forgetting, identical timing in SuperNI, multiple comparisons, conventional margins and untested larger models/optimizers. These should remain in the release.

Numerical scope: main internal tables and verdicts were traced to stored results and reproduced; this is not certification of every incidental count/date/reference or every upstream-paper quotation. The upstream-paper performance baseline (72.9), ablation numbers, bibliographic claims, GPU hours and reported historical chronology were not independently established from original external sources under the no-network rule.

### D: checks that passed

- The fully restored-state indistinguishability argument is sound under its stated assumptions: internal evidence restored identically cannot distinguish the histories. It does not prove the necessity of a particular physical clock.
- The anchor now separates non-restorable time from an operation counter and binds readings to a sealed state digest/lineage. With a trusted fresh path, a non-resettable counter and atomic commits covering counter and state, its comparison distinguishes advancement since the saved state from no committed advancement. Equal N plus elapsed time is correctly qualified as suspension **or execution without a commit**, not unique proof of suspension.
- Forks and cross-device copies are explicitly outside the simple sufficiency claim; the original instance need not detect a copy, and legitimacy requires external policy. No erroneous “physical irreversibility alone guarantees authenticity” claim was found; threat model C and physical-clock calibration/saturation limits are acknowledged.
- §5 correctly distinguishes non-inferiority closure, unresolved cost trade-offs and candidate non-identifiability; a rejected candidate does not close the level. Margins/costs must be fixed in advance, and a later level may use an earlier winning conventional mechanism. It explicitly admits that a complete evaluation contract and statistical procedure are not supplied. These are proposed tests, not an implemented programme already validated by Pilot 2.
- Pilot 2 is described as narrow evidence about gradient-updated states; memory/tool-state agents, costlier candidates, larger models and the continuity anchor remain untested. The abstract and body do not claim consciousness, moral status or personal identity results. The main inconsistency with the closure report is D01's stronger implication about residual information.

## Additional B scope notes and final limits

Synthetic-world source review also covered world.py, streams.py, metrics.py and the chunk/triplet constructors. Fact probes deliberately reuse trained items to measure retention; they are not claimed to be unseen-example generalization. Rule-task training/probe inputs are disjoint. Reference batteries R/B_beh are disjoint from target probes but **trained during childhood**, so the functional-distance comparison measures drift on a familiar reference battery. D3 and C10c/p/h use the same 8-step outputs but different functionals (sqrt-JS versus target loss/probability and entropy). This shared, low-complexity measurement setting may favour reconstruction; it is not proof of generic functional redundancy. Temporal covariates use t0 values and interval sums; no direct future forgetting outcome was found in the G2 predictor sets. The x_K_active NaN mask encodes which tasks have been evaluated, not their future loss values. The synthetic update norms are optimizer-derived by construction; no theorem makes all functional distances reducible to one moment norm.

What could not be checked:

1. Exact upstream FOREVER schedule, callback semantics, generation filters, training/data adaptation fidelity and whether the replacement strings affect only the intended upstream uses: the authors' source is absent and downloading it was prohibited. The patch itself was reviewed, not an executed upstream diff.
2. New model training, GPU generation, tie-diagnostic regeneration and CPU memory-fact rescoring from model weights: required weights/upstream data are absent from this package. Only stored outputs and the available generator source were checked. No unrelated local model/data installation was used.
3. Unpreserved historical source versions and whether local hash timestamps truly precede runs. Hash equality proves byte identity, not chronology or authorship. The 17 authors records, 1 external record and 3 lost records were classified, not verified.
4. Every bibliography entry, external performance baseline/quotation and literature-priority claim: no network was used. The Perspective's DOCX was not separately compared against its PDF; the closure PDF was not separately compared against the supplied Markdown.
5. Exhaustive numeric reconciliation of every narrative detail and exhaustive source correctness/formal proof. Main tables/verdicts are covered; full training and all potential configurations are not. The known lack of preserved source limits even an unlimited CPU reanalysis.
6. Independent earlier-review agreement: Recenzii and Colocviu verification documents were deliberately left unread. Findings here were derived without those reviews.
